# S-251X

**sound. on the go.**

a small music sketchpad for the browser. record a melody, give it a new
instrument, layer another part, and take the notes with you as MIDI.

`60 SEC` &nbsp; / &nbsp; `60–200 BPM` &nbsp; / &nbsp; `AUDIO → MIDI`

---

## 01 / what it does

- record a microphone take or import audio into a track.
- create, select, mute, duplicate, and delete tracks.
- play your arrangement with a tempo control and metronome.
- turn a clear single melody into piano, guitar, or electric-guitar notes.
- shift instrument parts up or down by octave.
- export unmuted instrument tracks as a MIDI file.
- keep the current arrangement available when you return in the
  same browser.

## 02 / make something

1. **set the pace.** drag the orange dial or move the tempo slider.
2. **record.** select a track and capture a phrase, or upload audio.
3. **change the voice.** switch to piano, guitar, or electric guitar; add a
   second track when you want a layer.
4. **listen.** press **Play** to hear the loop.
5. **take the notes with you.** **Export MIDI** when you want to continue
   elsewhere.

for the cleanest instrument conversion, use a clear solo melody. recording a
new take replaces the recorded part of the selected track; create another
track first to keep the earlier one.

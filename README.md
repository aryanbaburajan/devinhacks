# S-251X

**Sound. On the go.**

S-251X is a one-minute music sketchpad. Capture a melody, layer another part,
try it as piano or guitar, and take the result with you as MIDI.

## What it does

- Records a microphone take or imports audio into a track.
- Lets you create, select, mute, duplicate, and delete tracks.
- Plays your arrangement with a tempo control and metronome.
- Turns a clear single melody into piano, guitar, or electric-guitar notes.
- Shifts instrument parts up or down by octave.
- Exports unmuted instrument tracks as a MIDI file.
- Keeps the current arrangement available when you return to the app in the
  same browser.

## Make something

1. Set a tempo with the orange dial or slider.
2. Select a track and record a phrase, or upload audio.
3. Change its instrument and add another track if you want a layer.
4. Press Play to hear the loop.
5. Export MIDI when you want to continue elsewhere.

For the cleanest instrument conversion, use a clear solo melody. Recording a
new take replaces the recorded part of the selected track; create another
track first to keep the earlier one.
